# Cadence specification (from prototype v34)

Source: `added_docs/team_system_mobile_app_v34.html` (local design reference).

## Character limit

- Ideal max: **550** characters per answer
- Bands: ≤100 anemic, ≤350 concise, ≤550 ideal, ≤700 long, >700 verbose

## Daily

- Subtitle: Yesterday → Today → Blockers
- Questions: updates, today priorities, blockers
- Self Mission rating 1–5
- Parent targets: weekly priorities

## Weekly

- Subtitle: Report → Fixes → Roadmap
- Parent targets: monthly priorities
- Step 2: Team Weekly Priorities

## Monthly

- Subtitle: Bottlenecks → Systems → Roadmap
- Parent targets: quarterly priorities
- Step 2: Team Monthly Priorities

## Quarterly

- Subtitle: Strategic Reset
- Step 2: Team Quarterly Priorities

## Team report

- Ratings and comment thread shown on **daily** cadence only.
